✦ Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book for bridal services? +

We recommend booking at least 4–8 weeks before your wedding date. For peak wedding season (October–February), 3 months in advance is ideal to secure your preferred date and time.

Do you offer a trial makeup session before the wedding? +

Yes! We highly recommend a trial session 2–4 weeks before your wedding. The Royal Bride Bundle includes a complimentary trial consultation with your artist.

What products do you use? Are they skin-safe? +

We use premium, dermatologist-tested brands including MAC, HD Pro, Lakme Absolute, and Huda Beauty. All products are non-comedogenic and suitable for sensitive skin.

Do you provide at-home bridal makeup services? +

Yes, we offer at-home bridal service within Chalisgaon and nearby areas. An additional travel charge may apply for locations beyond a 10 km radius.
